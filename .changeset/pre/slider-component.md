Slider: new `et-slider` (single value) and `et-range-slider` (`[start, end]` tuple with `minDistance`) form controls, plus the headless `[etSlider]` / `[etRangeSlider]` / `[etSliderTrack]` / `[etSliderThumb]` directives and an `ng-template[etSliderThumbLabel]` value-label slot. Pointer drag with capture, full ARIA slider keyboard model, RTL-aware, signal-forms integrated (`SLIDER_IMPORTS`).
