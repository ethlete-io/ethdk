Bracket: `mirroredDoubleEliminationBracketLayout()` draws a double-elimination bracket as two stacked
blocks — the winners bracket folded around its own centre above the losers bracket folded around its
own, both centred on the same middle column, with each block's deciding rounds hanging in a vertical
chain below the round its two halves converge on. Also fixes a connector belonging to both of a match's
participants instead of the one who advanced along it, which lit the wrong journey on hover, and a
mirrored bracket's way back drawing its equal-match-count connectors backwards across a card.
