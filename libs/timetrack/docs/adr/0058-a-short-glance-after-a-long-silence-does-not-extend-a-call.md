# A short glance after a long silence does not extend a call

An open call is extended by a focus on the call window while its title still names the call. On 2026-11-23 a Discord
call ("Meeting #1", FIFAGG-12652) went silent at about 08:47Z. A focus on Vesktop of 1.6 s at 09:15:39Z, title still
"Meeting #1", extended the call, and the row grew from 15 to 45 minutes.

The human chose (German, verbatim): "q1ok", on the recommendation: a focus shorter than 5 s does not extend an open
call when more than 15 minutes passed since the call was last heard.

- A focus of 5 s or more still extends the call, and so does any focus within 15 minutes of the last heard sound.
- A real call keeps itself open through its sound and its transcript, not through glances at its window.
