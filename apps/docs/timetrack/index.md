# Timetrack

Timetrack is a desktop app that turns what you worked on into Tempo worklog drafts for Jira. It watches your own day on your own machine, builds draft worklog rows from it, and shows them to you. You review the rows and book them. Nothing is booked without your confirmation.

## Platforms

Linux (deb, rpm, AppImage), macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows.

## Download

Releases are published on GitHub as `timetrack-app@<version>` in the [ethlete-io/ethdk releases](https://github.com/ethlete-io/ethdk/releases). The app updates itself from there.

## Privacy

Timetrack keeps its data in an encrypted database on your machine. Read the [privacy policy](/timetrack/privacy) for what it collects and where data can go.
