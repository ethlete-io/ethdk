# Each part keeps its own view

After a switch in the part tree the canvas can show no frame at all: components/notification opened at 100 % on an
empty dotted area. The human (German, verbatim): "manchmal landet man beim navigieren zwischen dingen im tree auch
einfach im void und muss dann erstmal die frames suchen".

Asked with three options (always Fit; remember zoom and position per part, Fit on first open and when the remembered
view shows no frame; keep the zoom and jump to the first frame), the doc agent recommended the second. The human:
"q8 ok".

1. **Studio remembers zoom and position for each part.**
2. **Fit applies on the first open of a part, and when its remembered view shows no frame.** A switch in the tree never
   shows an empty canvas for a part that has frames.
