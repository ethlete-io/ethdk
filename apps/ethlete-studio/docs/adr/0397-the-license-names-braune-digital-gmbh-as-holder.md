# The license names Braune Digital GmbH as holder

The human, on the about dialog line "MIT-Lizenz, © 2025 The Authors": "2025 haben wir also? the authors?". The line
was the repo LICENSE's placeholder holder, copied verbatim.

- The repo LICENSE and the Studio about dialog carry the same copyright line, with the holder Braune Digital GmbH and the
  year span from 2025 to the current year.
- The about dialog computes the end year from the build year, so the line does not go stale.

The human, asked to choose between a real holder with a year span, a bare "MIT-Lizenz" without a copyright line, or no
change, with the recommendation "a real holder": "q1ok Braune Digital GmbH".
