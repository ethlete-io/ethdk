# A carousel with one slide shows no navigation

A carousel with a single slide still renders its control bar: previous and next arrows and one dot. The human sent a
screenshot of it and said "das carousel sollte keine navi zeigen wenns nur ein item hat".

A carousel with fewer than two slides renders no navigation: no arrows, no dots, and no control bar around them.
