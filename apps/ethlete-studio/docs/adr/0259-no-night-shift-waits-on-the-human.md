# No night shift waits on the human

At night the human answers no questions: "in der nachtschicht gilt generell dass ich keine fragen mehr beantworten
kann". This holds for every night shift, not only the night of 2026-11-10.

- Nothing at night waits on the human. A builder applies an existing ADR, or parks the item and takes the next one.
- The doc agent records findings, answers the builders and collects the questions for the human. It puts them to the
  human in one morning round.

The human: "q1ok" to this summary.
