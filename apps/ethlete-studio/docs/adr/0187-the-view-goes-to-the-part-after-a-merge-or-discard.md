# The view goes to the part after a merge or discard

When the open sketch is merged or discarded, it drops from the listing. The sketch view then leaves it and opens the
part the sketch targets, for example `pages/sketches`, and drops the old sketch frames. Tom can check at once that the
product looks like the picked variant. Tom: "q1ok" to the recommendation "zum Part".
